From Business Studies to Geography — and Professionally into Finance & Audit

My academic foundation began in Business Studies (SSC and HSC), where accounting, finance and business organisation first shaped my career interest. At honours and master's level I studied Geography & Environmental Science at National University — a research-heavy discipline built on statistics, field surveys, data collection, evidence analysis, mapping and structured reporting. Those are the same core skills used every day in audit evidence evaluation, depot and inventory verification, cost analysis and financial investigation.

Importantly, my connection with finance never stopped during those years: I worked in tax consultancy while studying, then as an accountant and as a research professional alongside my master's. I then committed fully to the profession — CA articleship (CA(cc), ICAB), statutory and forensic audit work for 30+ clients, cost optimisation training, and now an Executive MBA in Accounting. My discipline background is therefore different, but my professional qualification and 8.5 years of direct finance, audit and accounts experience are fully aligned with the roles I serve.

About the education timeline: there is no unexplained idle gap. Degree completion followed the National University academic calendar while I worked in parallel (see the timeline below), and every period since HSC is accounted for by study, employment or professional training.
